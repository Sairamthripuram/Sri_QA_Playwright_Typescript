FAQ

Questions people usually ask

Which course should I choose?

New to testing or no coding background? Start with AI Powered Manual Testing. Already testing and want automation? Choose Playwright with TypeScript — it's the most in-demand stack. Prefer Python or work on chatbot / AI products? Choose Playwright with Python & ChatBot. Still unsure — message us and we'll help.

Do I need prior coding experience?

No. The automation courses teach the language from scratch in Module 1, and the manual testing course needs no coding at all.

Are classes live or recorded?

Classes are live online and fully practical. Every session is also recorded so you can review it later.

What time are the classes?

Weekday batches run in the early morning IST, which works for students in India as well as the US and UK. See the next batch section above for the exact time in your time zone.

Is there job support?

Yes — career guidance, resume preparation and interview preparation are included with every course.

How do I pay, and what about USD pricing?

Fees are charged in INR. USD prices are approximate and vary with exchange rates. Message us on WhatsApp to enroll and we'll share payment details.

Can I switch or repeat a batch?

To keep training quality high, batch repetition and switching between batches or trainers isn't permitted once enrolled. Please plan to attend sessions regularly.

Not sure which course is right for you?

Send us a message — we'll recommend a path based on your background in a few minutes.
